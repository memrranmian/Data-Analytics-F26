Part F — Mathematical Expressions

1. Arithmetic Mean

The arithmetic mean represents the central tendency of a numerical feature distribution across $n$ observations:

$$\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$$

2. Classification Accuracy

Accuracy quantifies the proportion of correctly classified instances over the total population of evaluated predictions:

$$\text{Accuracy} = \frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN}$$

Where:

  • $TP$: True Positives
  • $TN$: True Negatives
  • $FP$: False Positives
  • $FN$: False Negatives
